LiveClin: evaluating medical AI with evolving clinical cases

Xidong Wang · 2026-10-15

Static benchmarks can become less informative as medical knowledge changes and evaluation examples enter training data. LiveClin explores a different approach: a live clinical benchmark built from contemporary, peer-reviewed case reports and updated biannually.

From questions to clinical pathways

The benchmark includes 1,407 case reports and 6,605 questions. Its multimodal evaluation scenarios span the clinical pathway, with a verified AI–human workflow involving 239 physicians.

Rather than evaluating isolated factual recall alone, these cases ask models to work through complex clinical scenarios.

What the evaluation shows

In the paper's evaluation of 26 models, the highest case accuracy was 35.7%. Chief physicians achieved the strongest performance, followed by attending physicians; both groups outperformed most evaluated models.

These results highlight the gap between strong benchmark knowledge and reliable performance across complete clinical cases.
