Xidong WANG

Ph.D. Candidate · CUHK-Shenzhen

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Benyou Wang. I received my B.Eng. from Beijing Institute of Technology as part of Xuteli School’s elite talent program.

My goal is to democratize progress toward AGI. I develop architectural and algorithmic methods that advance model capabilities while remaining efficient enough for researchers with modest resources to explore, contribute to, and build upon.

My earlier work in medical AI was motivated by the same goal: using AI for social good and making its benefits more widely accessible through open-source datasets, benchmarks, and multilingual medical models.

I am currently a research intern at IQuest Research.

Email: xidongwang1@link.cuhk.edu.cn

Google Scholar (2.0K+ citations)/GitHub/CV (PDF)

Research

I work on methods that improve capabilities and computational efficiency together, so that progress toward AGI is something a broader research community can participate in and extend.

  • Architectures and algorithms for stronger models. My work explores hybrid multimodal architectures (LongLLaVA), learning connections between Transformer blocks (T-Tuning), and strengthening reinforcement learning through value estimation and policy optimization (PPO+). T-Tuning and PPO+ are forthcoming work.
  • Earlier work: open, accessible, and scalable medical AI. Motivated by AI for social good, I developed methods and resources for scalable alignment (ClinAlign), evaluation (CMB), multilingual medical models (Apollo and ApolloMoE), and open medical datasets and models in the Huatuo series, including Huatuo-26M.
