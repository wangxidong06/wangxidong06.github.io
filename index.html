I am a Ph.D. candidate at CUHK-Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Benyou Wang. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology.

I work on large language models, with interests in mid-training and post-training, healthcare AI, and efficient multimodal learning. I am currently a research intern at Ubiquant IQuest Research, and previously interned at Ant Healthcare and the Shenzhen Research Institute of Big Data.

Feel free to get in touch to discuss research and potential collaborations.

Research

My research spans building capable models and evaluating how they perform in practical settings:

  • LLM training and alignment. Mid-training, post-training, and clinician-grounded principles for healthcare alignment.
  • Medical AI and evaluation. Clinical benchmarks and medical knowledge resources, including LiveClin, CMB, and Huatuo-26M.
  • Efficient and accessible models. Multilingual medical LLMs with Apollo and ApolloMoE, and long-context multimodal learning with LongLLaVA.
