Research

My goal is to democratize progress toward AGI. I develop architectural and algorithmic methods that advance model capabilities while remaining efficient enough for researchers with modest resources to explore, contribute to, and build upon:

  • Hybrid architectures for scalable multimodal learning (LongLLaVA)
  • Learning connections between Transformer blocks (T-Tuning, forthcoming)
  • Value-guided reinforcement learning for reasoning (PPO+, forthcoming)

My earlier work pursued the same goal through medical AI for social good, building open resources to broaden access:

  • Medical evaluation and leakage-resistant benchmarks (CMB, LiveClin)
  • Healthcare alignment from clinician preferences (ClinAlign)
  • Medical models for 50 languages (ApolloMoE)
  • Open medical datasets and models (Huatuo-26M, Huatuo Series)

See publications for the full list of my research papers.
